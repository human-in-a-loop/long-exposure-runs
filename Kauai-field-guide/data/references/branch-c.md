# Branch C — References (RARE + INVASIVE tier expansion, cycle 2)

Local reference file for the Branch C shard of cycle 2. Citations from
Branch C species YAMLs use tokens `"C:1"`, `"C:2"`, etc. — indices into
this file, which `scripts/build_site.py` remaps to global numbers at
render time.

[1] U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. (1996). Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and Plants; Determination of Endangered Status for Twenty-five Plant Species from the Island of Oahu, Hawaii, and Determination of Endangered or Threatened Status for Two Plant Species from the Island of Niʻihau, Hawaii. Federal Register (containing listing of *Panicum niihauense* as endangered).

[2] National Tropical Botanical Garden. Species profile: *Schiedea apokremnos* — cliff schiedea, Nā Pali coastal endemic. https://ntbg.org/database/plants/

[3] National Tropical Botanical Garden. Species profile: *Hibiscus waimeae* subsp. *hannerae* — Kokiʻo keʻokeʻo, endangered Nā Pali valley endemic. https://ntbg.org/database/plants/

[4] Wagner, W. L., & Weller, S. G. (2000). Revision of *Schiedea* (Caryophyllaceae, Alsinoideae). *Systematic Botany Monographs* 62: 1–108. (Includes description and habitat of *S. apokremnos* as a Nā Pali sea-cliff endemic.)

[5] University of Florida IFAS / Center for Aquatic and Invasive Plants; and multi-agency Hawaii weed compendia recording *Furcraea foetida* (Mauritius hemp) as naturalized in the Hawaiian Islands, including armed and unarmed cultivars.

[6] Wagner, W. L. & Herbst, D. R. (2003). Supplement to the *Manual of the Flowering Plants of Hawaiʻi*. Bishop Museum Occasional Papers 74: 1–58. (Contains post-1999 nomenclatural updates including *Schinus terebinthifolia* / *terebinthifolius* orthography.)

[7] U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. Species profile pages and ECOS records: *Kokia kauaiensis* (Kauai treecotton, listed endangered) — https://ecos.fws.gov/ecp/species/8488 ; *Chamaesyce celastroides* varieties (Kīlauea Point National Wildlife Refuge documentation and listing histories where applicable). Recovery plans and 5-year reviews accessed via ecos.fws.gov. (Cited by cycle-3 Branch C additions; specific version and access date recorded in the site image-credits log where individual USFWS pages are referenced.)

[8] Wagner, W. L., Herbst, D. R., & Sohmer, S. H. (1999). *Manual of the Flowering Plants of Hawaiʻi*, Revised Edition — Euphorbiaceae treatment of *Chamaesyce celastroides* including var. *stokesii* as a Niʻihau / Kauaʻi / Molokaʻi / Kahoʻolawe coastal endemic; and Kew Plants of the World Online, POWO, current APG-aligned placement of the taxon in *Euphorbia* as *E. celastroides* var. *stokesii*. Both names refer to the same plant. (Cited by chamaesyce-celastroides-stokesii.yaml for nomenclatural provenance.)

[9] U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. *Brighamia insignis* (ʻōlulu / ʻālula) 5-Year Review: Summary and Evaluation (2022). USFWS Pacific Islands Fish and Wildlife Office. Documents that no known wild individuals remain and formalises the extinct-in-the-wild status for USFWS-tracked recovery. https://ecos.fws.gov/ecp/species/1615 . A new 5-Year Review was initiated in September 2025.

[10] Walsh, S., Nyberg, B., & Wood, K. (2023). *Brighamia insignis*. The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species 2023 — assessment reaffirming category Extinct in the Wild (EW). https://www.iucnredlist.org/species/30598/207724488

[11] Heintzman, S., Nyberg, B., & Wood, K. (2020). *Kokia kauaiensis*. The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species 2020: e.T30934A83802016. Assessment category Critically Endangered (CR). https://www.iucnredlist.org/

[12] U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. *Kokia kauaiensis* (kokiʻo) 5-Year Review: Short Form Summary (2017). Documents decline from approximately 145 trees at 1996 listing to approximately 19 wild individuals. USFWS Pacific Islands Fish and Wildlife Office; ECOS species profile 8488. https://ecos.fws.gov/ecp/species/8488

[13] Hawaii Invasive Species Council (HISC). Invasive species profile: *Schinus terebinthifolia* — Christmas berry / Brazilian peppertree. Widely designated invasive; HISC alert list. https://dlnr.hawaii.gov/hisc/info/invasive-species-profiles/

[14] IUCN / SSC Invasive Species Specialist Group. Global Invasive Species Database entry for *Schinus terebinthifolius*, listing it among the world's damaging environmental weeds; PIER / Plant Pono corroborate high-risk designation for Hawaiʻi. http://www.iucngisd.org/gisd/species.php?sc=22

[15] Hawaii Administrative Rules, Title 4, Subtitle 6, Chapter 68 (HAR §4-68). List of Plant Species Designated as Noxious Weeds for Eradication or Control Purposes by the Hawaii Department of Agriculture. Statutory list (targeted eradication species — e.g., *Miconia calvescens*, *Passiflora tarminiana* / banana poka, *Hedychium gardnerianum* / kahili ginger). *Schinus terebinthifolia* is not on this statutory list as of the most recent codification consulted. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/hawaii/Haw-Code-R-tit-4-subtit-6-ch-68-List-of-Plant-Species-Designated-as-Noxious-Weeds-for-Eradication-or-Control-Purposes-by-the-Hawaii-Department-Of-Agriculture
