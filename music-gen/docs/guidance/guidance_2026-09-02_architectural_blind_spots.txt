OPERATOR ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTIVE (2026-09-02) — user listening verdict on the v2 gated winners (Chicken Grease drums, Peach Dream drums, Rome bass): "ALL OF THESE SAMPLES are still far off from the correct transcription." The v2 iteration passed its gates and still fails the ear. The operator audit concludes these are STRUCTURAL blind spots, not tuning issues. This directive supersedes threshold-tuning work on the current transcriber family. Seven blind spots, then four mandatory workstreams in priority order.

BLIND SPOTS IDENTIFIED:
(1) Gates measure statistical plausibility, never correctness, and the onset-F1 reference is CIRCULAR — it is computed against onsets from the same detector family that feeds the transcriber, so F1=1.0 means "kept its own input," not "found the drum hits." No gate anywhere compares output to ground truth.
(2) No musical time exists in the system: notes are absolute seconds; no tempo map, beat grid, or bars. Syncopation cannot even be represented, and the strongest structure in this music — the 1–2 bar loop repeated ~15x per 30s window — is never exploited. ~150 independent noisy per-onset decisions instead of 15 repeats voting on one pattern.
(3) Model-class ceiling: pyin, onset_detect+GMM, and basic-pitch are classical/lightweight tools near their ceilings on real separated stems; iterating their thresholds cannot recover what their representations never capture.
(4) The A/B verification lens conflates transcription error with timbre error: GM fluidsynth patches sound so unlike the source that even a perfect transcription is unverifiable by ear through them.
(5) Stems are transcribed independently despite separation bleed making them jointly dependent (the kick over-count WAS bass bleed); no cross-stem event reconciliation, no check that transcribed stems jointly explain the mix.
(6) Drum vocabulary {kick,snare,hat} is too coarse: open vs closed hat, ghost vs backbeat snare, toms, ride, crash are all forced into 3 clusters, corrupting the clusters AND the pattern. Bass ghost detector found 0 ghosts across all 5 songs — the class exists in the schema but the detector cannot see it.
(7) The peak-energy section is the densest, most bleed-contaminated input possible — maximally hostile to transcription; no easy-first curriculum.

MANDATORY WORKSTREAMS (priority order; all on the M-RECREATE-2 focus songs):
W1 — GOLD SET AS SOURCE OF TRUTH. Produce a reference transcription for 2–4 bars of the focus section of Chicken Grease and What If I Go: drums (full vocabulary incl. open/closed hat + ghost/backbeat snare distinction) and bass (pitches, octaves, rests, articulations). Build it by best-effort ensemble + careful manual listening-based correction by the researcher, document per-note confidence, and emit it for operator confirmation (the user will verify by ear via A/B). From then on EVERY transcription gate on these songs is accuracy vs gold (note-level F1 with pitch+class+50ms tolerance), not plausibility. Plausibility gates remain only as sanity checks.
W2 — MUSICAL TIME + REPETITION. Estimate tempo/beat/downbeat per song (survey: librosa beat_track vs madmom RNN if installable offline; pick by tap-test against the audio). Quantize onsets to a 16th-note grid WITH preserved micro-timing offsets (grid position + deviation ms, both stored in notes JSON). Detect the loop length (bar-level self-similarity) and aggregate evidence across repeats: same grid position across N repeats votes on class/pitch/presence. Emit both the aggregated loop pattern and the per-repeat deviations (fills/variations = where a repeat disagrees with the consensus).
W3 — LEARNED TRANSCRIBER SURVEY, REAL STEMS, OFFLINE-INSTALLABLE. Re-survey transcription with modern learned models, evaluated against the W1 gold set: drums — E-GMD/ADT-class models (e.g. Omnizart drum, OaF-drums ports); bass/vocals f0 — CREPE (torchcrepe); piano — ByteDance piano_transcription_inference; multi-instrument — MT3-class if weights are obtainable through the egress allowlist (GitHub releases pass; HuggingFace may pass — test). Classical DSP remains only as fallback where no learned model installs. Document install receipts + shas as always.
W4 — HONEST VERIFICATION RENDERS (concatenative resynthesis). For every A/B pair, ALSO render a version using material sliced from the song itself: cut each classified hit (kick/snare/hat/bass note) from the original stem via its onsets, build a tiny per-song sample bank (median/exemplar hit per class), and re-place samples at transcribed times (pitch-shift for bass via resampling). This isolates transcription accuracy from patch timbre and becomes the PRIMARY listening artifact sent to the operator; GM renders stay as secondary.
PLUS, integrated into the above rather than separate: cross-stem event reconciliation (a low transient claimed by both drum-kick and bass must be assigned or explicitly shared, using relative stem energy at the onset); widen drum vocabulary to at least {kick, snare, ghost-snare, closed-hat, open-hat, tom, cymbal} with per-song cluster count chosen by BIC not fixed k=3; and add ONE exposed/sparse section per focus song (verse or breakdown) alongside the peak section as an easier calibration case.
